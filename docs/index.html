Nature3D-AD: Geometry-Aware Feature Learning for Natural-Growth 3D Anomaly Detection

Paper Code Dataset

Data acquisition of NaturalGrowth. Each agricultural product is placed on a turntable and scanned from multiple views with an industrial structured-light scanner to obtain a complete 360° point cloud.

Abstract

Anomaly detection is an essential step in the agricultural production chain and plays an important role in food safety and consumer protection. However, existing point cloud anomaly detection benchmarks are designed for industrial scenarios, where normal samples share standard geometric shapes. Agricultural products grow naturally, so their shape, size and texture vary significantly, and "normal" is far less well-defined than for industrial parts. As a result, existing industrial benchmarks and methods are ill-suited for this setting.

To fill this gap, we build NaturalGrowth, the first point cloud anomaly detection benchmark for agricultural products. To handle the large feature variation, we propose GESA, which injects geometric priors at two levels: ASPE fuses local curvature and density into the position embedding to enhance the perception of anomalous regions, and GAA introduces geometric biases and a spectral branch into attention to strengthen local structure modeling. Experiments on NaturalGrowth, Real3D-AD and Anomaly-ShapeNet show that the proposed method achieves competitive performance.

Highlights

  • NaturalGrowth benchmark. 31 categories of common agricultural products with 1,477 real scans, covering natural defects (wormholes, rot) and physical damage (bruises, breakage), with point-level ground truth.
  • Anomaly-Sensitive Position Embedding (ASPE). Local curvature and density modulate a Fourier position embedding, so potentially anomalous regions receive a stronger positional signal.
  • Geometry-Aware Attention (GAA). Distance and normal-similarity biases are added to the attention scores, together with an FFT spectral branch for local structure modeling.
  • One model for all categories. A single unified model per dataset achieves the best average O-AUROC on NaturalGrowth, Real3D-AD and Anomaly-ShapeNet.

Framework

Overview of GESA. A frozen Point-MAE backbone extracts group features. ASPE enhances the position embedding with curvature and density cues, and stacked GAA encoder/decoder layers reconstruct normal features with geometric biases and a spectral branch. The reconstruction error gives point-level and object-level anomaly scores.
